# wishscene brand applications and review checklist

Version 0.1 · Applies to marketing, product UI and exported media

## Landing page structure

1. **Hero:** “Imagine it. Make the scene.” plus a labelled example set showing one person in several connected moments. CTA “Create a scene.”
2. **Proof of difference:** one shared story plan keeps face, outfit, place and mood consistent across formats. Avoid absolute quality claims until tested.
3. **How it works:** add photos, choose a place, direct scenes, approve outputs, download pack.
4. **Formats:** photos, Stories, carousel, short clips, Reel and editable captions. Mark video as “coming later” until Phase 2 ships.
5. **Trust:** consent, private storage, deletion controls and AI-created export disclosure in plain language.
6. **FAQ:** “Can I use someone else's photos?” “Are my photos used to train models?” “Can I delete everything?” Answers must match implemented provider/data policies, not assumptions.

## App surfaces

**Onboarding:** use human examples only with permission. Show source-photo quality cues and privacy summary before upload.
**Storyboard:** the biggest decision point; show chronology and continuity facts, not just a prompt box.
**Review:** let the person compare candidate to their own selected references privately; never use public examples as proof of identity.
**Export:** preview crop, caption and disclosure together. Provide a manifest and clear file names. If platform metadata can be stripped, do not promise it will persist after upload elsewhere.

## Social launch templates

Announcement: “Meet wishscene. Pick a place you can imagine, direct a few moments, and turn them into one connected creative story starring you. Photos first; video follows as we test consistency.”

Build update: “We tested whether four photos looked like the same person in the same evening. Here’s what worked, what failed, and what we’re changing.” Share aggregate results or consent-cleared examples only.

Demo video outline (20–30 seconds): idea -> editable four-scene storyboard -> candidate review -> carousel/Reel pack -> AI-created label. Use actual product capture once available; conceptual mockups must say “concept.”

## Consistency checklist for any asset

- Product name lowercase `wishscene`.
- Shows creative imagination, not verified real-world attendance.
- Uses palette/type and readable contrast; works on mobile and RTL where relevant.
- Human likeness has permission; examples are labelled AI-created.
- Product capability and availability claims match the shipped version.
- CTA names the next action; loading/error/privacy language is direct.
- No fake EXIF, GPS, receipts, tickets, timestamps, or “proof” messaging.

## Before public launch

Finalize logo assets, check trademark/domain/app-store conflicts with appropriate counsel, validate landing copy with target users, set actual retention/provider disclosures, and update screenshots to match the built app. The documents are an actionable design brief, not completed visual assets.
