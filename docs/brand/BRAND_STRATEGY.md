# wishscene brand strategy

Version 0.1 · 27 September 2026 · Working brand, ready for team exploration

## Brand in one line

**wishscene turns an imagined experience into a coherent set of photos, short videos, and social stories starring you.**

The name is always written **wishscene** in lowercase in product copy, URLs and wordmarks. GitHub repo: `coders-clan/wishscene`. This guide establishes a working direction, not a trademark clearance or final visual identity.

## Positioning

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| Category | Personal AI experience studio |
| Audience | Adults creating playful visual stories with their own likeness |
| Job | “Show me in an imagined place or moment, across media that feels like one story” |
| Difference | A versioned story plan keeps identity, wardrobe, place, time and mood consistent across photos, clips and posts |
| Emotional payoff | Explore a possibility and share a creative version of yourself |
| Functional payoff | One editable storyboard produces a complete, exportable content pack |
| Trust promise | Consent for every likeness; clear AI creation and provenance |

**Positioning statement:** For people who want to picture themselves somewhere else, wishscene is an AI experience studio that turns an idea into a consistent, editable photo and video story. It keeps the same person and scene facts across every output, so the whole pack belongs together.

## The brand idea

**Make the scene you wish existed.**

A “wish” supplies imagination. A “scene” makes it tangible. The product is a creative studio, not a travel tracker, evidence maker, or social network. It should feel playful, visually rich and in the user's control.

## Brand pillars

1. **You remain you.** Recognizable identity is the first quality requirement, with explicit approval by the subject.
2. **One world, many formats.** The storyboard is shared by photo, clip, Reel, Story and caption.
3. **Directable imagination.** The user can choose, adjust, reject and repair; the product explains what changes.
4. **Honest creation.** The result may be photorealistic, but it is framed and exported as AI-created media.

## Messaging hierarchy

**Primary:** “Imagine it. Make the scene.”

**Explanation:** “Create a whole visual story starring you—from photos to short videos and posts—set wherever you imagine.”

**Proof:** “Your face, outfit, place and timeline stay consistent across the experience.” This is a product target; marketing must not claim it is achieved until evaluation gates pass.

**Call to action:** “Create a scene.” Secondary: “See how it works.”

Other copy options for tests:
- “Anywhere looks better with you in it.”
- “A place in your mind. A story on your screen.”
- “One idea. A whole scene.”

Avoid “I was there,” “prove you went,” “fool your friends,” “real travel photos,” “undetectable,” and guarantees such as “perfect likeness.”

## Audience and contexts

Primary use: dream destinations, alternate-day adventures, imaginative celebrations, playful creative personas. Creator use: storyboarding consistent personal content. Avoid default templates that imply public figures, official events, crises, endorsements, or unconsenting people. The initial audience is adult and single-person; group stories require separate consent design.

## Brand personality

Imaginative, warm, confident, observant, lightly witty. Never smug, manipulative or hyperbolic. The user is the director; the app is a capable creative partner. Aim for “a thoughtful photo editor with a sense of wonder,” not a prank product.

## Naming and architecture

- Company/product: **wishscene**.
- Experience: one user-created imaginative story.
- Scene: one moment inside an experience.
- Storyboard: ordered scene plan.
- Story Bible: internal versioned continuity record; in UI say “Scene details” or “Story settings.”
- Content pack: exported photos, clips, social formats and captions.
- Director mode: possible later advanced editor; do not advertise before shipping.

The product name has had only a quick web exact-match search. Before public launch, commission proper trademark, domain, app-store and regional language review. Do not represent this document as legal clearance.

## Success signals

Unaided users understand that output is imaginary and AI-created; users can say what makes wishscene different from a single-image generator; finished packs feel coherent and like the user; trust language reduces confusion about photo storage and sharing. Validate via five-second comprehension tests and interviews before paid acquisition.
